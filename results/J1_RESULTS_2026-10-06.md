## Analyses
- Stage-wise U (S1): most of the loss occurs at the final update (CDF): seq 87.50 → 87.87 → 86.17; GAD+LwF 88.21 → 88.38 → 87.88.
- 32-frame rescoring agrees with 8-frame results (AA within 0.3; no-update reproduces the Lightning test 98.21 / 92.04).
- Feature drift (22 runs incl. sweeps): retention is predicted by representation stability, Spearman ρ = −0.95 between
  genuine-feature drift and ΔU, but fake-feature drift correlates equally (−0.93 to −0.96); partial correlation of
  genuine drift given fake drift −0.22. Drift does not by itself isolate a genuine-specific mechanism (the controls M1/M2 do).
  GAD lowers genuine drift relative to fake drift (genuine/fake-stream ratio 0.68 GAD vs 0.75 symmetric, random-subset
  and fake-only; 0.69 GAD+LwF vs 0.79 symmetric+LwF).
- Calibration (32 frames, ECE 15 bins): unseen panel ECE GAD+LwF 0.081 vs seq 0.118 (better); trained-task test ECE
  GAD+LwF 0.072 vs seq 0.040 (worse); mean genuine FPR@0.5 on trained tasks 9.7% vs 4.3% (less adaptation).
