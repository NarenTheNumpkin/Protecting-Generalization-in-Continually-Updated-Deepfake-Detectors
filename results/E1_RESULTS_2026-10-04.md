## Pre-specified questions
- **Q1 asymmetric vs symmetric distillation (both without replay):** realkd − featkd FSh = +2.15 / +1.73 / +2.15,
  AA +0.24. **Consistent in all seeds**; the Gate 2 effect was not a replay artefact.
- **Q2 does prototype replay add anything?** realkd − radial FSh = +0.14 / −0.24 / +0.06; AA identical.
  **No.** The vMF memory and real-anchored drift compensation contribute nothing measurable here; the
  effect of "RADIAL" comes entirely from real-only distillation.
- **Q3 radial + LwF:** FSh vs lwf +2.59 / +1.76 / +2.69; vs radial +2.20 / +2.45 / +1.81 (positive in all seeds).
  It keeps 74% of what seq loses on FSh (loss 2.0 vs 7.8), has no seen-task forgetting (AF ≈ 0) and
  the best FF ACC@0.5 (91.1). AA guardrail met (98.26 vs 98.42 − 0.5). **The learning guardrail is missed
  by 0.002** (gain 1.048 vs threshold 1.050): it learns new tasks at half the rate of seq. Per task:
  DFDCP +1.17, DFD +1.21, CDF +0.77 (seq: +1.49, +2.79, +2.02).

## Interpretation (exploratory)
1. The robust, simple mechanism is **real-only (genuine-anchored) feature distillation**: distil the
   previous model's embedding on genuine faces only, leaving fakes free. It beats symmetric distillation
   by about 2 FSh points with equal or better seen-task metrics, in every seed tried (6/6 across Gate 2 and E1).
2. Real-only feature distillation and logit distillation (LwF) are complementary (+about 2 points over either).
3. The prototype memory and drift compensation (the "exemplar-free memory" part of the original RADIAL
   proposal) is unsupported by these data and should be dropped from the method, or reported as a
   negative ablation.
4. There is a clear stability–plasticity trade-off: the most protective variant learns new tasks least.
5. All of this is on the dev FaceShifter probe that guided the design. Confirmation needs the sealed
   unseen panel, the clean retrained init, and the sealed test splits.
