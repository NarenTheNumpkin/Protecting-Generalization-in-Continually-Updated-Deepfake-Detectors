## Reading
1. **Main result.** With few-shot updates on three new datasets, realkd_lwf keeps unseen-forgery generalisation
   essentially at the no-update level (U 87.88 vs 87.97, −0.09), while plain updating loses 1.80 and LwF 1.09.
   At the same time it improves the new tasks over no update (test AA 94.78 vs 93.27: DFDC-P +2.5, CDF +2.5,
   DFD +0.8) and does not degrade the base task (FF 98.32; FF ACC@0.5 93.7 vs 84.7 for seq).
2. **Mechanism.** Genuine-only feature distillation beats symmetric distillation on the unseen panel
   (+0.49, CI excluding 0), smaller than on the dev probe (+2.0) but in the same direction.
3. **Where generalisation is lost.** The loss is concentrated on fully synthetic faces (DFF text2img: 78.0
   → 65.2 for seq; realkd_lwf keeps 73.8). On real-world WildDeepfake, *every* update slightly helps
   (90.9 → 91.8–92.4), so updates mainly hurt detection of unseen *generation families*.
4. **Trade-off (honest).** realkd_lwf learns the new tasks less than seq (DFDC-P 88.2 vs 90.8) and has a
   slightly lower test AA than realkd (94.78 vs 95.23), within the registered guardrail.
5. **Limitations.** Single training seed (Amendment C1-A), so CIs capture test-sample but not training-seed
   variance. One task order (S1). Few-shot regime only. Method variants were selected on dev data
   (Gate 2/E1/E2) before this confirmation. DFD reals use largest-face crops.
